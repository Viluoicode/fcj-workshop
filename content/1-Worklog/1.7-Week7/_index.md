### Week 7 Objectives:

* Understand the differences between relational and NoSQL databases.
* Learn the basic architecture and use cases of Amazon RDS.
* Learn the fundamental concepts and use cases of Amazon DynamoDB.

### Tasks to be carried out this week:

### Week 7 Achievements:

* Connected with other interns and exchanged learning plans for the database study week.
* Reviewed the foundational documentation and architecture needed before studying Amazon RDS and DynamoDB in greater detail.
* Understood the basic difference between relational and NoSQL databases.
* Understood the main components of Amazon RDS.
* Understood the purposes of backups, snapshots, Multi-AZ, and Read Replicas at a conceptual level.
