This worklog records my 24-week internship in two phases of 12 weeks each.

**Phase A (Weeks 1-12) - AWS theory and foundations:** structured learning of core AWS services, hands-on labs, and events.

**Phase B (Weeks 13-24) - Projects:** Project 1 (Weeks 13-20) is Job Market AWS Pipeline; Project 2 (Weeks 21-24) is OnTime Radar (Shipment SLA Service), alongside the close-out of Project 1. Project 1's first prototype was started in late June 2026, before the internship began on 03/07/2026, and its Git commit history reflects that. During Phase B, I re-scoped, redesigned, hardened, and deployed it on AWS using the knowledge from Phase A (Terraform, Bronze/Silver/Gold, Data Quality, Step Functions, monitoring, testing, and documentation).
